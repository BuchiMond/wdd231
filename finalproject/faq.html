FAQ List

After you submit your project brief through the contact form, we review your goals, brand assets, and timeline. We then send a project proposal with scope, pricing, and a start date. Once approved, we begin with concept development, share initial drafts, and refine based on your feedback before delivering final files.
All standard packages include two rounds of revisions. A revision round means you provide consolidated feedback and we apply all changes in one pass. If you need additional rounds, they can be added at a flat rate. We find two rounds is enough for most clients when feedback is clear and specific.
You receive all files required for your specific deliverable. For logos and branding this includes SVG, PNG (transparent background), PDF, and the native source file (AI or PSD). Print items are delivered as print-ready PDFs at 300 dpi with bleed. Digital assets are exported at appropriate web resolutions.
Turnaround depends on the service — each listing on our home page shows the estimated timeline. Simple items like a flyer take 1–2 business days; complex projects like a full brand identity take 10–14 days. Timelines begin once we receive all required content and materials from you.
We require a 50% deposit before work begins, with the balance due upon delivery of final files. For larger projects (over $500), we offer milestone-based payment schedules. We accept bank transfer, PayPal, and major credit cards. Final files are delivered after payment is confirmed.
Yes. Rush delivery (typically 24–48 hours) is available for most services and carries a 40% surcharge. Availability depends on our current workload — mention your deadline in the project brief and we'll confirm if rush is possible before you commit.
Once the final invoice is settled in full, all rights to the finished design transfer entirely to you. You are free to use, modify, print, and reproduce the work without restriction. We retain the right to display the project in our portfolio unless you request otherwise in writing.
For most projects, yes — you provide the text content and any specific photos or brand assets you already own. We can source licensed stock photography and write basic copy for an additional fee. The clearer and more complete your content brief, the faster and better the outcome.
Head to our Contact page and fill in the project brief form. Let us know what you need, your budget range, and your deadline. We'll respond within one business day with next steps and a quote.
